Plain-language definitions
CNNA neural network designed to find visual patterns in images.
Head classA category with many training examples.
Tail classA category with relatively few training examples.
RecallOf the real examples in a class, the share the model finds.
Overall accuracyThe share of all predictions that are correct; common classes influence it most.
Average class recallAlso called balanced accuracy: average recall across classes, giving every class equal influence.
