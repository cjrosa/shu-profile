What is a weight?

A weight represents how strongly one piece of information influences the next step.

Key takeaway A neural network does not understand the decision. It combines numerical inputs through layers of weighted connections to produce a pattern-based prediction.
Why it matters The result depends on the training examples, labels, selected inputs, network design, and patterns the system learns from the data.
