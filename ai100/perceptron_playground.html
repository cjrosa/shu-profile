Why one neuron gets stuck

Try different settings. Fixing one situation will eventually break another because a single perceptron can make only one simple dividing rule.

Every perceptron makes a decision in the same way: it multiplies each input by its weight, adds the results, and answers Yes when the total reaches its threshold. The network passes Yes as 1 and No as 0.
