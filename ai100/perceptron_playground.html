A perceptron in everyday life

Should I go to Sally’s Apizza?

Use a familiar decision to see how a simple AI system combines information.

Learning goal See how a decision changes when every opinion counts equally versus when some opinions carry more weight.
What is a perceptron? A perceptron is a simple artificial neuron: a numerical decision maker that combines inputs and applies a rule.
Every person counts equally: yes is +1, no is −1, and a positive total wins.

Calculation
Majority rule More Yes votes than No votes → Go

Key takeaway A perceptron does not understand pizza. It combines numerical inputs using weights and a decision rule.
Why the setup matters The result depends entirely on the inputs, weights, and threshold chosen by a person or learned from data.
