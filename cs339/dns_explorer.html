    Record basics & model notes

    A maps a hostname to an IPv4 address. CNAME maps an alias to another hostname. MX identifies a mail server; its activities come in the email tool.

    The domain names are familiar examples. All addresses, aliases, TTLs, and server data here are fictional teaching data, not the live DNS configuration of Google or Sacred Heart University. Time advances only with +61 s; playback illustrates order, not latency.

    The local resolver sits outside the DNS hierarchy. Usually it follows iterative referrals; the laptop asks it recursively. Fully recursive forwarding through the root and TLD is a conceptual comparison. Only in that comparison do these nodes cache forwarded answers in this model. Owned delegations and authoritative records are separate from caches. Device caches, failures, replicas, and cross-domain aliases are omitted.
