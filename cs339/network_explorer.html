What this model includes

Explicit Ethernet ports, broadcast and unknown-unicast flooding, MAC learning, ARP, and one ICMP Echo exchange per ping.

Access VLANs isolate broadcast domains on each switch. Click an interface to assign its VLAN or enable/disable it. VLAN 1 is the default; access frames are untagged. Routers connect subnets using connected routes and host default gateways. Trunks, DHCP, DNS, and NAT come later. Layer 2 loops are blocked until loop handling is introduced. Timings are illustrative simulation ticks, not measured latency.
